Fix these before the pilot

  1. A citizen can see another citizen's name and phone after their reports are merged (H1).
  2. Two coordinators can take the same grievance at once, and both are told it worked (H2).
  3. Grievances logged at the MLA office can never be closed (H4).
  4. A failed voice-note transcription writes a fake "Large Pothole" title and routes it to the wrong department (H5).
  5. Merging can fold a fresh complaint into an already closed one, and merged reports keep appearing as live grievances (H6, H7).
  6. Disabling a coordinator hides their open grievances from everyone (H8).

All six are small, contained fixes, mostly on the server. H3 (acting on another office's grievance by ID) matters most when Chennai is live with many offices; it is cheap to close at the same time.

Information people will miss

Small gaps in what the screens show. The data is already there; only showing it is missing.

Coordinator

  • Who reported it and their phone number. The server sends both, but the app never shows them, so a coordinator cannot call the citizen.
  • A status badge on Open cards. A transferred or walk-in grievance looks the same as a fresh report.
  • Colleagues' names instead of internal IDs like @arunkumarddfa in messages.
  • Tapping a notification only dismisses it; it should open that grievance.
  • Which department or officer a grievance was transferred to, on the card itself.
  • In the duplicate review: whether the original is still open, closed or false.

Citizen

  • When it was assigned and when it was resolved. Only the filing date is shown.
  • The department it went to, and the coordinator's note (for example, why it was marked false).
  • After rejecting a fix: "You rejected this on …, the coordinator has been asked to review". Today the card silently turns back to "Assigned".
  • A "Your report" marker in the ward feed.
  • Tapping a notification should open that grievance.

Not for v1

  • Uploading several photos per grievance: for v1, limit the picker to one photo (see M14) rather than building multi-photo upload.
  • The real Tambaram ward → assembly constituency split: the office covers all 70 wards as one group until the official list arrives.
  • Per-action notification types, and letting several coordinators share one grievance: design decisions for after the pilot.

Method: three code reviews in parallel (coordinator logic, citizen journeys, status and notification consistency), then every server-side finding replayed through the API on a scratch copy of the backend. App-only behaviour (lists, labels, location) was confirmed in code, and the no-GPS behaviour was also seen in the iOS simulator earlier.
