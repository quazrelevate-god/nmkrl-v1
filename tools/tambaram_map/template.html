Needs info

Which constituency is each ward in?

This map shows wards and zones only. The app groups wards by assembly constituency, so we need the official ward-to-constituency list for Tambaram (it spans more than one constituency).

Needs a decision

Ward numbers repeat Chennai's

Tambaram's wards 1–70 share numbers with Chennai's wards. Either run Tambaram as its own setup, or label wards with a prefix such as “TMB 12”.

Good to know

Borders are close, not exact

Checked against 15 landmarks (4 railway stations, 11 lakes) plus MIT, Vels, the National Institute of Siddha and Thiruneermalai temple. Borders sit within about 40–100 m, so a complaint filed right on a border may land in the next ward.

Good to know

The drawing covers 78.1 km², not 87.64

The printed map says 87.64 km² (the official figure), but its drawn boundary measures 78.1 km². The scale is pinned by stations 9 km apart, so this gap is in the drawing itself. We did not stretch the map to hide it.
